The End

The End is a place of darkness and silence. Its lands stretch endlessly into the unknown, cold and empty yet somehow filled with the presence of something far greater.

It is a realm where everything feels distant, where the familiar rules of the world begin to lose their meaning. The End represents the finality of all things. Nothing can last forever, no matter how powerful, ancient, or enduring it may seem.

Architect

Elythara, the Architect of the End, is the oldest of the Five Architects. They existed before the other realms were shaped, their power predating the world itself. Ancient and unknowable, Elythara is a force beyond the understanding of those who live within the realms they created.

Aspect

Ender + Evocation

The Aspect of the End draws its power from two forces deeply intertwined with the realm: Ender and Evocation.

Its wielders channel strange and powerful magic, drawing upon forces that blur the boundaries between what is possible and what should be. The Aspect is unpredictable and difficult to fully understand, reflecting the nature of the End itself.
