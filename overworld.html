The Overworld

The Overworld is a place of change. Whether it's day or night, hot or cold, or the weather, something in the Overworld is always changing, always growing.

Architect

Sylvara, the Architect of the Overworld, is a force of nature and lightning. She is a caring and nurturing Architect, often seen as the mother of all life. She watches over the Overworld and its people with a deep connection to the living world, guiding growth and change wherever she can.

Aspect

Nature + Lightning

The Aspect of the Overworld is one of growth and change. It draws upon the living world around you, allowing its wielders to influence nature and call upon the power of storms.

Those touched by the Overworld may find their magic taking many different forms. Some may nurture life, manipulate plants, or shape the land around them. Others may call upon lightning and the power of the storm.

How your Aspect manifests is unique to you, but all who wield it share a connection to the Overworld and its Architect.
