The Otherside

The Otherside is a place of darkness and miasma. Its lands are cold and still, filled with strange life that does not belong anywhere else. Something always seems to linger in the air, spreading quietly through the land and changing everything it touches.

Architect

Miazomal, the Architect of the Otherside, is a force of disease and corruption. He is an unsettling and ancient figure, viewed with unease by the other Architects. Yet within his own realm, he is deeply protective of the things that call it home. To Miazomal, nothing in the Otherside is unnatural. It is simply another form of life, and he wants nothing more than to see it spread.

Aspect

Eldritch

The Aspect of the Otherside draws its power from things that should not exist. It is strange, unpredictable, and difficult to understand, shaped by the ancient forces that dwell beyond the boundaries of the other realms.

Those touched by the Otherside may find their magic taking forms that are difficult to explain. Their power may twist, distort, or change the world around them in ways that feel unnatural to those who have never seen it before.

The Eldritch Aspect is not meant to be understood. It is meant to be experienced.
