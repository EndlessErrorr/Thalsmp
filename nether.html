The Nether

The Nether is a place of heat and decay. Its lands are harsh and unforgiving, filled with fire, smoke, and life that seems to linger long after it should have died.

Architect

Cinichor, the Architect of the Nether, is a force of blood and fire. His realm is one of survival, destruction, and decay. He is a quiet and imposing figure, his presence carrying an ominous weight. To Cinichor, destruction is not an ending, and decay is not something to be feared. Both are simply parts of the natural order.

Aspect

Blood + Fire

The Aspect of the Nether draws its power from two forces deeply intertwined with the realm: blood and fire.

Its wielders can manipulate the heat and flame of the Nether, while blood magic allows them to draw upon life itself as a source of power. The Aspect is destructive, but it is also about survival. Those touched by the Nether learn to endure, even in a world that seems determined to consume them.
