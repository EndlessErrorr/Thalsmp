The Aether

The Aether is a place of stillness and spirit. Its skies are vast and bright, its lands are cold and peaceful, and everything feels touched by something beyond the mortal world.

Architect

Nivener, the Architect of the Aether, is a figure of reverence and judgment. She is elegant and distant, watching over the Aether with a quiet authority. Her warmth is not easily found, but neither is her cruelty. She believes that everything has its place, and those who stray from it may find themselves judged.

Aspect

Holy + Ice

The Aspect of the Aether draws its power from two forces deeply intertwined with the realm: holy magic and ice.

Its wielders draw upon the divine and the cold, channeling the reverence of the Aether and the stillness of ice. The Aspect is powerful, but it is also restrained. Those touched by the Aether learn to wield their power with purpose, carrying with them a connection to the divine and to the Architect who governs their realm.
